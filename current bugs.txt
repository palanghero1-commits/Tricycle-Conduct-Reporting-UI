I found these important bugs and risks:

  ### Critical

  - Unauthenticated database seeding — POST /seed has no login or admin check. Anyone can seed the database and
    receive the demo password.
    api-php/index.php:711

  - Weak complaint access control — complaint_or_404() only checks ownership for students. A driver or reviewer
    can potentially access complaints outside their assigned TODA if they know the complaint ID.
    api-php/index.php:738

  - TODA scope is not enforced — TODA presidents can update statuses, add actions, and manage violations for
    complaints belonging to other TODAs.

  - Default TODA assignment bug — Driver accounts created without todaId are automatically assigned to TODA 1,
    which may be wrong or may not exist.
    api-php/index.php:349

  ### Functional bugs
